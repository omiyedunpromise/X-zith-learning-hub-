🎯 What is X-ZITH?

X-ZITH is an AI-powered learning platform designed specifically for Nigerian students. We combine cutting-edge artificial intelligence with educational expertise to help students prepare for national examinations including WAEC, NECO, and JAMB.

Our platform provides personalized study materials, AI-powered tutoring, practice questions, and real-time feedback to help you achieve academic excellence.

Get AI-generated study notes for any topic in seconds. Perfectly formatted and easy to understand.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is X-ZITH really free to use? +
Yes — X-ZITH is completely free for students. There are no hidden costs, subscriptions, or ads.
Which exams does X-ZITH prepare me for? +
X-ZITH focuses on WAEC (WASSCE), NECO (SSCE), and JAMB (UTME), with study notes also covering the JSS curriculum.
Does it work on a basic phone or slow internet? +
Yes, X-ZITH is built to work smoothly even on low-bandwidth connections and entry-level smartphones.
Can the AI read notes and questions aloud? +
Yes — study notes, past questions, theory questions and explanations, and AI Tutor replies all have a 🔊 voice button for listening on the go.
How do quizzes work? +
After generating study notes on a topic, tap "Take Quiz" for 5 AI-generated questions, each with a 20-second timer and an explanation after you answer.
