❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is X-ZITH completely free?

▼

Yes! X-ZITH is 100% free for all Nigerian students. No hidden charges, no subscriptions. Study as much as you want!

Does it work for WAEC and JAMB?

▼

Yes! We have real past questions from WAEC, NECO, and JAMB. Practice with actual exam questions to boost your confidence.

Can I use it without internet?

▼

X-ZITH works as a PWA (Progressive Web App). Once you load it once, you can use basic features offline. Just download the app to home screen!

What subjects does Fixto cover?

▼

Over 20 Nigerian curriculum subjects! Mathematics, English, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, History, Geography, Civic Education, and many more. If your subject isn't listed yet, request it!

How do badges and points work?

▼

Earn badges by reaching score milestones: 🎉 Welcome (0 pts), ⭐ Rising Star (500 pts), 📘 Bright Scholar (1500 pts), 🏅 Achiever (3000 pts), 🏆 Champion (5000 pts), 💎 Elite Mind (8000 pts), 👑 Legend (10,000 pts)!

Can teachers track student progress?

▼

Yes! The leaderboard shows top students, and activity logs track all quiz attempts and scores. Perfect for classroom challenges and competitions!
