🎯 What is X-ZITH?

X-ZITH is an AI-powered learning platform designed specifically for Nigerian students. We combine cutting-edge artificial intelligence with educational expertise to help students prepare for national examinations including WAEC, NECO, and JAMB.

Our platform provides personalized study materials, AI-powered tutoring, practice questions, and real-time feedback to help you achieve academic excellence.

✨ Key Features

📖 Smart Textbook Generator

Get AI-generated study notes for any topic in seconds. Perfectly formatted and easy to understand.

🤖 AI Tutor (Fixto)

Ask anything, get instant answers. Our AI tutor never gets tired and is always available 24/7.

📝 Past Exam Questions

Practice with exam-style questions modelled on WAEC, NECO, and JAMB papers. Generate up to 60 questions at a time.

🌐 Web Search

Find YouTube videos, articles, and tutorials for any topic instantly.

🏆 Leaderboard

🔊 Voice Support
