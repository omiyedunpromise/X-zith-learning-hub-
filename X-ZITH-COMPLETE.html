ℹ️ About X-ZITH Learning Hub

🎯 What is X-ZITH?

X-ZITH is an AI-powered learning platform designed specifically for Nigerian students preparing for WAEC, NECO, and JAMB exams. We provide personalized, adaptive learning experiences powered by artificial intelligence to help you achieve your academic goals.

🚀 How to Get Started

  1. Create Account: Sign up with your email and create a password
  2. Explore Textbooks: Generate AI-powered study notes for any topic
  3. Ask Questions: Use our AI Tutor to get instant answers
  4. Practice Exams: Access past exam questions and test yourself
  5. Track Progress: Monitor your scores and compete on the leaderboard

  • Built for Nigerian Students: Focused on WAEC, NECO, and JAMB syllabuses
  • Accessible: Works on low-bandwidth connections and basic phones
  • Affordable: Completely free - no hidden costs
  • Culturally Relevant: Examples and explanations tailored to Nigerian context
  • Community Driven: Compete with peers and share knowledge

📚 Features

  • Textbook Generator: AI generates comprehensive study notes instantly
  • AI Tutor: Ask anything and get instant explanations
  • Past Questions: Practice with real exam questions from past years
  • Quiz Maker: Auto-generated quizzes to test your knowledge
  • Voice Reading: Hear all content read aloud
  • Web Search: Find videos and resources instantly
  • Leaderboard: Compete with other students nationwide
  • Progress Tracking: Monitor your learning journey

🛠️ Technology Stack

  • AI Models: Gemini 2.5 (primary), DeepSeek (exam questions), OpenRouter (fallback)
  • Voice: Text-to-Speech for all content
  • Search: SerpAPI for finding resources
  • Backend: Node.js + Express
  • Database: PostgreSQL (Neon)
